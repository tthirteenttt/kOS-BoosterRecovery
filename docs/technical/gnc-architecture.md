## Core Flight Control & Guidance Subsystems

### 1. Pre-Flight 3D-RK4 Digital Twin (`estimate.ks`)
Before liftoff, `estimate.ks` strips launch-clamp mass from the live vessel and integrates a coupled 3D-RK4 trajectory across Stage 1 gravity turn, staging, Stage 2 delta-V budget, 3-engine entry burn, aerodynamic glide, and terminal hoverslam. It verifies Stage 1 propellant margin, Max-Q, and Stage 2 orbital insertion margin, then writes the optimal ASDS landing coordinates directly to `0:/config/asds_predicted.ks`.

### 2. Ascent & Closed-Loop UPEG (`ascent.ks`)
- **Launch Window & Azimuth**: Solves 3D inertial launch azimuth (including Earth rotation) and optionally rails-warps to a `FIXED` LAN plane (`lan_window.ks`, BetterTimeWarp compatible).
- **Stage 1 Gravity Turn**: Power-law pitch schedule guarded by dynamic-pressure-scaled FAR AoA clamping (`MAX_AOA_DEG`) and liftoff RCS interlock.
- **Stage 2 UPEG**: After manual interstage separation and a 2.0 s RCS ullage clearance hold (`ST_S2_CLEARANCE`), closed-loop Unified Powered Explicit Guidance steers Stage 2 to `< 1 km` insertion accuracy across LEO, SSO, MEO, and GTO.

### 3. Booster Recovery FSM (`recovery.ks`)
```text
ST_FLIP ─▶ [ST_BOOSTBACK (RTLS)] ─▶ ST_COAST ─▶ ST_ENTRY_BURN ─▶ ST_AERO_GLIDE ─▶ ST_LANDING_BURN ─▶ ST_TOUCHDOWN
```

- **Stowed FAR Grid-Fin Deflection Lock/Unlock (`gridfin_mixer.ks`)**: Because KRE's stock `ModuleDeployableAero` lock does not recognize FAR's `FARControllableSurface`, `ascent.ks` injects `Ctrl Deflect = 0` prior to liftoff so stowed fins produce zero aerodynamic torque during ascent; `recovery.ks` automatically re-arms `Ctrl Deflect = 25` upon deployment in `ST_COAST`.
- **Dynamic-Pressure-Gated Post-Entry RCS (`rcs_controller.ks`)**: During `ST_AERO_GLIDE`, once dynamic pressure exceeds `RCS_Q_CUTOFF` (`0.09 atm`), forged titanium grid-fin aerodynamic torque completely dominates attitude control and cold-gas RCS is switched off (with hysteresis re-enable below `0.06 atm` and during low-speed hoverslam deceleration).
- **Coupled TVC-Over-Aero Hoverslam (`hoverslam_guide.ks`)**: Accounts for powered subsonic normal force (`CN_ALPHA_LAND = 3.9`) and retropropulsion drag (`CD_POWERED = 0.14`), gates lateral guidance by net TVC-over-aero polarity margin, extends landing legs at `180 m` radar altitude, and locks upright below `12 m` for sub-meter precision touchdown.
- **Hybrid `EngineValue` RCS Safety Guard**: Guards `SHIP:RCS` iteration with `ISTYPE("RCS") AND HASSUFFIX("ENABLED")` so payloads carrying hybrid `EngineValue` RCS modules (e.g., `MSL.CS.REACluster`) never trigger kOS suffix exceptions.
